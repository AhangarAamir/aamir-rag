## Default and cure

**MF-8.3** The defaulting Party shall have **twenty-one (21) days** from receipt of the default notice to cure the default.

*(Executed 2018 JOA Article 8.3 provides **thirty (30) days**. Deviation: JOA is **looser / more generous** to the defaulting party by 9 days.)*

## Assignment notice

**MF-12.1** A Party proposing to assign shall give the other Parties **forty-five (45) days’** prior written notice.

*(Executed 2018 JOA Article 12.1 requires prior written consent not to be unreasonably withheld, but **does not** specify a 45-day notice period. Deviation: **missing** model-form notice period.)*

## Cost recovery cap

**MF-21.2** Cost recovery shall not exceed **60%** of the value of Petroleum produced and saved in a Year.

*(Executed JOA Article 21.2: **80%**. PSC Article 16.2: **70%**. Playbook is tighter than both.)*
