**JOINT OPERATING AGREEMENT**

Block: **NEC-OSN-97-2 (NEC25)**  
Effective Date: **1 August 2018**  
Governing Production Sharing Contract: PSC dated 12 April 2000, as amended on 15 March 2016  

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made on 1 August 2018 among:

1. **Horizon Energy India Limited** (“Horizon”), a company incorporated in India;
2. **Northern Basin Petroleum Pte. Ltd.** (“NBP”); and
3. **Coastal Oil Corporation** (“Coastal”).

The Parties are collectively the “Contractor” under the PSC.

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

**(a) “Petroleum”** means crude oil, condensate, and natural gas produced from the Contract Area, and **includes** coal bed methane, following the 15 March 2016 amendment to the PSC. For the avoidance of doubt, the unamended 2000 PSC definition of Petroleum **excluded** coal bed methane. Disputes arising from operations conducted **before 15 March 2016** shall apply the unamended definition.

**(b) “Participating Interest”** means a Party’s undivided percentage interest in the rights and obligations of the Contractor under the PSC and this Agreement.

**(c) “Operating Committee”** means the committee constituted under Article 5.

**(d) “Discovery”** has the meaning given in Article 3.2 of the PSC.

**(e) “Investment Multiple”** or **“IM”** means the ratio calculated in accordance with Appendix C of the PSC.

## ARTICLE 8 — DEFAULT

**8.1** A Party is in default if it fails to meet a cash call or other financial obligation under this Agreement. Whether a default has occurred shall be determined in accordance with **Article 8.2**.

**8.2** The Operator shall issue a written default notice. The notice period shall be as stated in **Article 8.3**.

**8.3** The defaulting Party shall have **thirty (30) days** from receipt of the default notice to cure the default. If the default is not cured within that period, the remedies of the non-defaulting Parties are set out in **Schedule 4**.

## ARTICLE 12 — ASSIGNMENT

**12. Assignment of Interest**

**12.1** No Party shall assign all or any part of its Participating Interest without the prior written consent of the other Parties, not to be unreasonably withheld, and without any approval required under the PSC.

**12.2** Any assignment shall be documented by an assignment letter stating the interest before, the interest after, and the effective date.

**12.3** An assignment that has not received required Government approval is **void as against the Parties** until such approval is granted.

## ARTICLE 16 — APPRAISAL PROGRAMME FOLLOWING DISCOVERY

**16.1** Where a Discovery is made, the Operator shall submit an appraisal programme to the Operating Committee and to the Government **within one hundred eighty (180) days of the date of Discovery**, or such longer period as the Government may allow.

**16.2** For illustration only: if a Discovery occurs on **12 January 2024**, the appraisal programme is due on **10 July 2024**, unless the Government grants a longer period.

**16.3** “Days” in this Article means calendar days, not Business Days.

## ARTICLE 19 — ROYALTY AND PROFIT PETROLEUM

**19.1** Royalty shall be paid in accordance with the PSC and any notification issued by the Government from time to time.

**19.2** Until superseded, royalty under Article 19 of the PSC is **ten percent (10%)** of the well-head value of Petroleum produced and saved.

**19.3** The Parties acknowledge MoPNG Notification dated **4 June 2026** (sample), which provides that royalty shall be **twelve and one-half percent (12.5%)** of well-head value **with effect from 4 June 2026**. From that date, **Article 19.2 is superseded** to the extent of conflict. Operating Committee minutes dated 18 June 2026 which continue to refer to 10% do **not** prevail over the Notification.

**19.4** Profit petroleum shall be shared between the Government and the Contractor according to the sliding scale in **Annex E**, by reference to the Investment Multiple for the relevant Year.

## ARTICLE 21 — COST RECOVERY

**21.1** The Contractor may recover Contract Costs from Cost Petroleum in accordance with Article 16 of the PSC.

**21.2** Cost recovery in a Year shall not exceed **eighty percent (80%)** of the value of Petroleum produced and saved in that Year, unless the PSC provides otherwise.

**21.3** Comparison note for testing: JOA Article 21 is intended to be read with PSC Article 16. If the PSC and this JOA differ on the cost-recovery cap, **the PSC prevails**.

## SCHEDULE 4 — REMEDIES ON UNCURED DEFAULT

**S4.1** If a default is not cured within the period in Article 8.3, each non-defaulting Party may elect, by notice, to require the defaulting Party to offer its Participating Interest for purchase (a “buy-out”).

**S4.2** The buy-out price shall be calculated in accordance with the formula in **Annex D**.

**S4.3** During the buy-out period, the defaulting Party shall have no vote on the Operating Committee in respect of operations funded by the unpaid cash call.

---

## ANNEX D — BUY-OUT FORMULA

**D.1** Buy-out Price = (Defaulting Party’s Participating Interest) × (Net Book Value of Joint Property) × 0.90.

**D.2** Net Book Value shall be taken from the last audited statement approved by the Operating Committee.

---

## ANNEX E — PROFIT OIL / PROFIT GAS SPLIT (SLIDING SCALE)

The Contractor’s share of Profit Petroleum, and the Government of India (GOI) share, for a Year shall be:

| Investment Multiple (IM) for the Year | GOI share of Profit Petroleum | Contractor share of Profit Petroleum |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 1.5 | 10% | 90% |
| Equal to or greater than 1.5 and less than 2.5 | 25% | 75% |
| Equal to or greater than 2.5 and less than 3.5 | 40% | 60% |
| Equal to or greater than 3.5 | 50% | 50% |

**E.1** Bands are **inclusive at the lower bound and exclusive at the upper bound**, except the top band which has no upper bound.

**E.2** Example: if IM is **2.7**, the applicable band is “equal to or greater than 2.5 and less than 3.5”, so GOI **40%** and Contractor **60%**.

**E.3** The Contractor share is then allocated among the Parties in proportion to their Participating Interests as of the last day of that Year.

**E.4** This Annex E is the scale in force from the Effective Date (1 August 2018). A prior 2005 scale (not attached) shall not be applied to Years after the Effective Date.
