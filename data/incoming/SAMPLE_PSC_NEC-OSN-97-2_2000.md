**PRODUCTION SHARING CONTRACT**

Block: **NEC-OSN-97-2 (NEC25)**  
Date: **12 April 2000**  
Parties: The President of India (the “Government”) and the Contractor.

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1 “Petroleum”** means crude oil, condensate, and natural gas produced from the Contract Area. **Petroleum does not include coal bed methane.**

**1.2 “Contract Area”** means Block NEC-OSN-97-2.

**1.3 “Year”** means a period of twelve months commencing on 1 April (Indian fiscal year), unless the context requires otherwise.

---

## ARTICLE 16 — COST RECOVERY

**16.1** The Contractor may recover Contract Costs from Cost Petroleum.

**16.2** Cost recovery in a Year shall not exceed **seventy percent (70%)** of the value of Petroleum produced and saved in that Year.

**16.3** Unrecovered Contract Costs may be carried forward.

---

## ARTICLE 19 — ROYALTY

**19.1** The Contractor shall pay royalty at **ten percent (10%)** of the well-head value of Petroleum produced and saved.

**19.2** Royalty shall be as notified by the Government from time to time. A later notification prevails over this Article to the extent of conflict.

---

## APPENDIX C — INVESTMENT MULTIPLE (2000 SCALE)

This scale applies to Years **before 1 August 2018**:

| Investment Multiple | GOI share | Contractor share |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 1.5 | 5% | 95% |
| 1.5 to less than 2.5 | 15% | 85% |
| 2.5 to less than 3.5 | 30% | 70% |
| 3.5 or more | 45% | 55% |

From 1 August 2018, profit petroleum is shared under **Annex E of the 2018 JOA**, not this Appendix C.
