## ARTICLE 1 — AMENDMENT TO DEFINITIONS

**1.1** Article 1.1 of the PSC is deleted and replaced as follows:

**“Petroleum”** means crude oil, condensate, natural gas, **and coal bed methane** produced from the Contract Area.

**1.2** Operations and disputes relating to periods **before 15 March 2016** continue to apply the unamended 2000 definition (coal bed methane excluded).

---

## ARTICLE 2 — NO OTHER CHANGE

**2.1** Royalty under PSC Article 19 remains **10%** of well-head value unless a later Government notification provides otherwise.

**2.2** Cost recovery under PSC Article 16 remains subject to the **70%** annual cap.
